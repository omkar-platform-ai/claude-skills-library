# fitness-advisor

Personalised fitness coaching across training, nutrition, recovery, and
supplementation. Supports muscle gain, fat loss, body recomposition, and
long-term health for a non-novice audience.

## When it triggers

- "Build me a workout plan", "I want to gain muscle / lose fat"
- Nutrition / kcal / protein guidance
- Plateau diagnosis, deload planning, periodisation
- Supplement questions tied to a specific goal
- Programme adjustments under medical or schedule constraints

## Inputs it expects

- Age, weight, height, training experience
- Goal (hypertrophy, fat loss, recomp, performance)
- Weekly training time available
- Any medical constraints (with clear deference to physio / doctor)
- Tracking habits (kcal, weight, sleep)

## Output

- Structured plan with sets, reps, rest intervals, and progression rules
- Daily kcal and macro targets tied to body composition goal
- Recovery, sleep, and tracking advice
- Phased programme (e.g., 12-week block with deloads) when the goal warrants it
