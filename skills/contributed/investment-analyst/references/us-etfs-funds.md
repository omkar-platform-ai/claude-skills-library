# US ETFs & Mutual Funds — Evaluation Reference

## Instrument-Specific Dimensions

### Categories

1. **Broad Market Index ETFs** — S&P 500, Total Market, QQQ
2. **Factor ETFs** — Value, Growth, Momentum, Quality, Low Volatility
3. **Sector ETFs** — Tech, Healthcare, Financials, Energy, etc.
4. **Bond ETFs** — Short/Intermediate/Long Duration, TIPs, HY
5. **International ETFs** — Developed Markets, EM, Single-Country
6. **Thematic ETFs** — AI, Clean Energy, Robotics, etc.
7. **Active ETFs** — Actively managed, transparent holdings
8. **Mutual Funds** — Active and passive, share class matters

### Key Metrics to Assess

**Performance:**

- Total return vs benchmark (1Y / 3Y / 5Y / 10Y)
- Rolling returns consistency vs category
- Dividend yield and total return distinction
- Tax-loss harvesting friendliness (for taxable accounts)

**Risk:**

- Annualized standard deviation
- Beta vs S&P 500
- Max drawdown (2000 dot-com, 2008 GFC, 2020 COVID, 2022 rate shock)
- Sharpe and Sortino ratios (3Y, 5Y)

**Operational:**

- Expense Ratio:
  - Broad index ETFs: <0.05% ideal; >0.20% is expensive for passive
  - Factor ETFs: <0.25% reasonable; >0.50% needs strong justification
  - Active ETFs: <0.75% reasonable; >1.0% needs very strong case
- AUM: >$1B preferred for ETF liquidity; <$100M = closure risk
- Bid-ask spread: <0.05% for liquid ETFs; watch for illiquid thematic ETFs
- Tracking error vs benchmark: <0.10% for index ETFs; flag >0.25%
- Turnover: Low preferred for tax efficiency in taxable accounts

**Structural:**

- Physical vs synthetic replication
- Securities lending income (offsets expense ratio)
- Distribution frequency (monthly/quarterly/annual)
- Authorized participant quality and liquidity

### Tax Treatment (US — Resident)

- **Short-term capital gains (<1 year):** Taxed at ordinary income rate
  (up to 37%)
- **Long-term capital gains (>1 year):** 0% / 15% / 20% depending on
  income bracket
- **Qualified dividends:** 0% / 15% / 20% (same brackets as LTCG)
- **ETFs vs Mutual Funds:** ETFs are more tax-efficient due to in-kind
  creation/redemption
- **Index ETFs:** Minimal capital gains distributions; highly tax efficient
- **Bond ETFs:** Interest income taxed as ordinary income
- **Municipal Bond ETFs:** Interest exempt from federal tax (and often
  state tax)
- **TIPS:** Inflation adjustments taxed as ordinary income (even if
  unrealized — phantom income)
- **International ETFs:** Foreign tax credit may apply; check fund's
  pass-through policy

**For NRIs investing in US funds:**

- Subject to 30% withholding tax on dividends (unless tax treaty applies)
- India-US DTAA: reduced rate may apply; consult tax advisor
- PFIC (Passive Foreign Investment Company) rules apply for US funds
  held by non-residents — extremely complex; flag this and recommend
  professional tax advice

### Account Type Optimization (US)

- **Taxable account:** Prefer index ETFs, tax-managed funds, municipal bonds
- **Traditional IRA / 401(k):** REITs, bonds, high-turnover active funds
  (tax-deferred)
- **Roth IRA:** Highest expected return instruments (growth ETFs, small cap)

### ETF Selection Criteria (Minimum Bar)

- AUM >$500M
- Expense ratio at or below category median
- 3+ years track record
- Daily volume >$10M (liquidity)
- Tracking error <0.15% for index funds
- No imminent closure risk signals (low AUM trend)

### Red Flags — Avoid ETFs With

- AUM <$100M with declining AUM trend (closure risk)
- Tracking error >0.30% without explanation
- Expense ratio >2x category median
- Illiquid underlying (some small-cap international, frontier markets)
- Recently launched thematic ETF (<2 years old) with pure momentum/hype driver
- Leveraged / inverse ETFs for buy-and-hold (not for long-term portfolio)

### Popular Benchmarks

- US Equity: S&P 500 (SPY/VOO/IVV), Total Market (VTI), Nasdaq-100 (QQQ)
- International Developed: MSCI EAFE (EFA)
- Emerging Markets: MSCI EM (EEM/VWO)
- US Bonds: Bloomberg US Aggregate (AGG/BND)
- Global All-Asset: MSCI ACWI (ACWI/VT)

### US Mutual Funds — Additional Considerations

- Share class matters: Institutional (lowest cost) > Investor > Advisor
- 12b-1 fees: Flag any fund with ongoing distribution fee
- Load vs No-load: Recommend no-load only
- Minimum investment: Can be a barrier for smaller investors
- Prefer ETF equivalent when available and more tax efficient
